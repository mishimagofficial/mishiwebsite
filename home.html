Issue #1 Froghop
A Place in Time

Welcome to Froghop, the first edition of Mishi Mag! Here you'll find stories, experiences, and art from different pockets of the world - from big cities to university towns. We wanted to take snapshots of different scenes and subcultures in time, as well as shout out and showcase some great artists.

From it's synthesis in all the way to now, Froghop has been on a journey of change and so have the scenes that can be found within its pages. This magazine has become a time capsule and a love letter to the past few years. Check out our first edition out now.
